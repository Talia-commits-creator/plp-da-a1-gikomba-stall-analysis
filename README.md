## Project Overview

This project analyses 14 days of sales and stock records for Wanjiku, a fresh-produce vendor at Gikomba Market. The objective is to evaluate business performance, identify sales growth trends, classify daily performance, compare weekday and weekend sales, and determine which products need restocking. Using core Python, the analysis provides practical insights to support better inventory management and business decisions.

## How to Run

Open the notebook in Google Colab: [Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/Talia-commits-creator/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

## Top Three Findings

1. Total sales were KES 46,450, with Week 2 recording 10.66% growth over Week 1.
2. Weekend average sales were KES 4,900, approximately 82.50% higher than the weekday average of KES 2,685.
3. Tomatoes and Sukuma need restocking because their current stock levels are below their reorder points.
